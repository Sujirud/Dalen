Why Dalen

The finance app built for real life, not spreadsheets.

Six principles that guide every feature we build—from the algorithm to the interface.

01

Truly Dynamic Budgeting

Unlike static monthly budgets, your daily limit recalculates every morning based on what you actually have and what's coming up—so the number is always honest.

02

Goal-First Architecture

Your savings goal isn't a side feature—it's the engine. Every spending limit, every forecast, every nudge is calculated with your goal deadline at its core.

03

Effortless Logging

Receipt scanning and one-tap transaction entry mean logging your spending takes less time than the purchase itself. No friction, no backlog.

04

Privacy by Default

Your financial data never leaves your control. No ads, no selling your habits to third parties. Dalen earns your trust with every feature we don't include.

05

Insightful Analytics

The dashboard gives you a real-time net worth overview and an interactive chart comparing your daily budget limit against actual spending.

06

Built to Start in Minutes

No bank connections, no complicated setup, no sync errors. Add your balance, set a goal, and your first daily limit is ready before your morning coffee cools.
